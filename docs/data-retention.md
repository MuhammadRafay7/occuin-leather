# Data retention

This records what the OCCUIN store keeps today, and what the owner still has to decide. No period below has had legal review.

## Removed automatically
| Data | When |
|---|---|
| Expired sessions | At the next sign-in by any user |
| Expired password reset links | At the next reset request |
| Rate limit counters | About a day after their window ends |
| Expired bespoke links | When the next bespoke link is issued. Links last 90 days, and 14 days once a request is declined or completed |

## Removed when a customer deletes their account
Account, sessions, reset links, saved addresses, reviews, wishlist, bag, saved designs, logos, newsletter entry and contact messages sent from that email. Bespoke requests with no order are removed.

Deletion is refused while an order is open (pending, paid, in production, shipped) or a bespoke request is accepted or in production.

## Kept after account deletion
| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Orders, including email and shipping address, detached from the account | Tax, accounting and returns records |
| Bespoke requests tied to an order, with name, email, phone, notes, messages and reference images removed. The design stays | Record of what was made and sold |

## No automatic removal yet
Orders, order addresses, guest bags, guest designs, bespoke requests, bespoke images, logos, contact messages and newsletter entries stay until someone removes them.

## Decisions for the owner, with legal advice
1. How long to keep orders and their addresses in each country sold to, and whether addresses can be removed earlier than the financial record.
2. Whether keeping the customer email on orders after account deletion is justified.
3. How long to keep bespoke images, logos and messages when no order follows.
4. How long to keep guest data, contact messages and newsletter entries, and whether the newsletter needs double opt-in.
5. Whether UK GDPR, EU GDPR or Pakistani law applies to each market, and who answers data requests.
6. Whether reviews should be deleted or anonymised on account deletion. They are currently deleted.
